# Prompt per generare i quattro badge

Da usare con un generatore di immagini. Scritti in inglese perché i modelli
rispondono meglio, e senza nominare marchi esistenti.

Sono proposte di evoluzione, da presentare accanto al logo originale, non al suo
posto. Palette: terracotta #C75B4A, miele #E3BE72, crema #F7F0E4, inchiostro
#2B2724, salvia #6E8F85.

## Regole valide per tutti

Aggiungi sempre in coda al prompt:

```
flat vector logo, solid colors only, no gradient, no drop shadow, no 3D,
no photorealism, no text unless specified, centered composition,
transparent or flat background, high contrast, print-ready
```

E in negativo, se il tuo strumento lo prevede:

```
gradient, glossy, chrome, bevel, drop shadow, 3d render, photo, realistic,
watermark, signature, blurry, lowres, extra limbs, distorted face, cartoon,
mascot, caricature
```

Il generatore sbaglia quasi sempre il testo: **genera il simbolo senza lettere**
e aggiungi il nome dopo, col font vero, in un editor vettoriale.

## 01 · Timbro

```
Minimal flat vector emblem: a circular disc in dusty terracotta red, with the
side profile silhouette of a Native American man facing left, cut out in cream
negative space inside the disc. A simple soft-gold headband stripe crosses his
forehead, with two long stylized feathers angled back and up from the band. A
single braid line curves down behind. Thin cream inner ring border. Dignified
and respectful, calm expression, no caricature. Bold geometric shapes, flat
solid colors: terracotta #C75B4A, cream #F7F0E4, honey gold #E3BE72.
Vintage American club badge, 1950s sign painter style, clean and modern.
```

## 02 · Linea

```
Minimal single-weight line-art emblem: a thin circle outline containing the side
profile of a Native American man facing left, drawn with one continuous elegant
stroke. A headband stripe in terracotta red, two simple feathers angled back, a
braid line curving down. Uniform stroke width throughout, no fill, no shading.
Dignified, calm, respectful, no caricature. Colors: dark ink #2B2724 line on
cream background #F7F0E4, one terracotta accent stroke.
Engraving style, suitable for gold foil stamping on metal.
```

## 03 · Monogramma

```
Bold geometric monogram logo: a heavy sans-serif letter Y where the two upper
arms end in two stylized feather shapes pointing up and outward, one terracotta
red and one honey gold. A thin horizontal band crosses the Y at mid height. A
small dot below the stem. Extremely simple, flat, symmetrical, no face, no
figure. Cream shapes on very dark brown background. Strong silhouette that stays
readable at 16 pixels. Contemporary brand mark, confident and minimal.
```

## 04 · Badge

```
Flat vector badge: a circular emblem, upper two thirds filled with a large honey
gold setting sun disc, lower third crossed by two simple sage green wave lines
suggesting the North Sea. In front of the sun, the side profile silhouette of a
Native American man facing left in solid dark ink, backlit against the sun, with
a terracotta headband stripe and two feathers angled back. Thin dark outer ring.
Dignified, respectful, no caricature. Colors: honey gold #E3BE72, dark ink
#2B2724, terracotta #C75B4A, sage #6E8F85, sand #F1E4CC.
Seaside club crest, flat and modern, no gradient.
```

## Poi

Quello che esce è una base, non un logo. Portalo in un editor vettoriale
(Figma o Inkscape, entrambi gratis), ridisegna i contorni con curve pulite,
allinea le proporzioni e crea le varianti: orizzontale, verticale, favicon,
un colore solo, positivo e negativo.

Un logo generato e usato così com'è si riconosce subito: curve irregolari,
spessori che cambiano, dettagli che non reggono a 16 px.
